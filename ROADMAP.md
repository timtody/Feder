# Feder roadmap

The roadmap lives in Linear: workspace outerspace, team **Feder** (`FED`), <https://linear.app/outermostspace/team/FED/all>.

- **Releases** are projects (Feder 1.2, in progress).
- **Ideas** are issues labelled `Differentiator` (features that set Feder apart), `P3` (bigger bets) or `Moonshot`;
  product decisions to make first are labelled `Decision`.
- **Suggested next** is what sits in Todo, highest priority first.

Everything stays on the phone; where a feature touches the network, iCloud or another app, its issue says so, since that
is a product decision. What users already got is in `CHANGELOG.md`. The last markdown version of this file:
`git show 346475a:ROADMAP.md`.
